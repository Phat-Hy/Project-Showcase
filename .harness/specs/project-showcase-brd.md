# Problem Statement: Gara Startup Project Showcase

## Problem Statement
Within university startup incubators like "Gara," valuable physical infrastructure and funding are dedicated to fostering student innovation. However, once teams enter incubation, their daily operational progress, pivot decisions, and changing resource needs disappear into a "black box."

Because there is no centralized, real-time portal to visually display active projects, the entire ecosystem operates under extreme information asymmetry. The lack of a standard, media-rich platform prevents student startups from broadcasting their active minimum viable products (MVPs), pitch decks, or recruitment requirements, rendering active innovation virtually invisible to the broader campus and external partners.

## Affected Users
The digital silence and communication barriers within the Gara incubator compromise three primary user cohorts:

*   **Student Startup Founders & Core Teams:** They are isolated within their own academic departments. Tech-heavy teams struggle to recruit business, marketing, or design talent from other faculties, resulting in critical skill gaps that stall development.
*   **The General Student Body (The Potential Talent Pool):** Thousands of students across diverse disciplines are eager to join early-stage projects to build portfolios or secure internships. However, they face a high barrier to discovery with no central feed to browse active projects or apply.
*   **Incubator Managers, Mentors, and University Administrators:** They are tasked with monitoring project health and allocating grants but lack visibility. They are forced to actively chase teams across disjointed chat apps (Zalo, Messenger, Discord) to manually piece together status updates.

## Impact
The absence of an open, continuous tracking mechanism results in severe operational and institutional consequences:

*   **High Project Mortality Rates:** Many student startups quietly dissolve within their first 3 to 6 months due to preventable, unaddressed talent shortages or because mentors discover structural business flaws weeks after a crisis occurred.
*   **Capital and Resource Waste:** The university dedicates limited lab equipment, prime workspace, and grant funding to projects that have silently stalled or abandoned their development goals.
*   **Interdisciplinary Siloing:** Rather than creating a collaborative, cross-faculty incubator, departments continue to operate as isolated islands, losing the synergy that drives successful real-world startups.

## Root Cause / Contributing Factors
This chronic friction persists due to several foundational structural factors:

*   **Absence of a Unified Digital Gateway:** There is no centralized Content Management System (CMS) tailored to student projects, forcing teams to waste precious time building standalone landing pages or relying on private messaging threads.
*   **Static and Retrospective Reporting Frameworks:** The incubator relies on administrative, time-bound manual reporting (e.g., monthly Excel spreadsheets or slides) rather than continuous, milestone-driven updates.
*   **Decentralized Talent Matching:** No integrated channel exists to match project openings with student credentials, forcing teams to rely strictly on immediate friend networks for hiring.
## Vision and Objectives
## Vision
The Gara Startup Project Showcase exists to transform the university's physical incubator into a vibrant, decentralized digital ecosystem. By replacing isolated departmental silos with an open, media-rich gateway, the platform democratizes startup visibility. It turns invisible, localized student innovation into a transparent network of shared resources, enabling any student—regardless of their faculty—to discover, collaborate on, and drive real-world startup projects forward.

## Objectives
*   **Foster Cross-Disciplinary Synergy:** Break down the barriers between the Engineering, Business, and Design faculties, encouraging students of different disciplines to co-create and exchange intellectual capital.
*   **Optimize Talent Allocation:** Build a friction-free internal talent marketplace that immediately connects active student startup projects with skilled, motivated peers across campus.
*   **Implement Data-Driven Governance:** Transition university administrators and mentors from retrospective, manual reporting methods to active, real-time portfolio management.

## Success Metrics & KPIs
To ensure these objectives are met with high accountability, the platform's performance is measured across three core pillars:

### Ecosystem Vitality (Platform Engagement)
*   **Active Project Update Rate:** Target of 100% of active Gara teams updating their project profiles, media galleries, or interactive timelines at least once every 14 days.
*   **Monthly Active Users (MAU):** Target of 50% growth quarter-over-quarter in unique student visitors. (needs_confirmation)
*   **User Retention & Stickiness:** Achieve an average session duration of over 3 minutes per visit.

### Sourcing & Matchmaking (Interdisciplinary Recruiting)
*   **Cross-Faculty Hiring Rate:** Target of 25 successful cross-faculty placements annually (e.g., Business or Design majors fully embedded into technical founding teams). (needs_confirmation)
*   **Application Conversion Volume:** Tracking the total volume of student CVs submitted through the integrated recruitment module to monitor student interest.

### Operational & Administrative Efficiency
*   **Digital Reporting Adoption:** Achieve 100% digital progress-tracking adoption, completely replacing manual monthly spreadsheets or presentation deck updates with the interactive, asynchronous milestone timeline.
*   **Mentor Intervention Response Time:** Reduce the window for a mentor to identify and react to a delayed or "at-risk" project milestone to under 48 hours.

## Scope and Capabilities
## Scope
The Gara Startup Project Showcase is a centralized digital ecosystem designed to replace isolated departmental silos with an open, media-rich gateway. The system scope is restricted to providing a **Centralized CMS Layer** for startup visibility, an internal talent matching marketplace, and an administrative oversight dashboard. All secondary features are deferred to maintain delivery focus and platform simplicity.

## Capabilities
| Capability | Priority | Rationale | Dependency |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Self-Serve Profile Creator** | P0 | Enables startup visibility and professional documentation. | SSO Authentication (needs_confirmation) |
| **Rich-Text & Media Gallery** | P0 | Essential for showcasing product prototypes and demos. | CMS Backend |
| **Asynchronous Milestone Tracker** | P0 | Eliminates manual reporting; ensures continuous tracking. | CMS Backend |
| **Role Posting Board** | P1 | Enables cross-faculty talent recruitment. | Project Profile |
| **Simplified Application Flow** | P1 | Lowers barriers for students to join startups. | Role Posting Board |
| **Aggregate Project Overview** | P1 | Provides real-time visibility for incubator managers. | Project/Milestone Data |
| **Proactive Milestone Alerts** | P2 | Enables early-stage intervention for at-risk projects. | Milestone Tracker |
| **Automated Report Exports** | P2 | Streamlines administrative reporting cycles. | Aggregate Data |

## Out of Scope
*   **Real-time Instant Messaging:** No internal chat; teams use external tools (Zalo, Discord).
*   **Financial/Grant Payment Processing:** No integrated payment gateways for funding.
*   **AI Resume Screening:** No automated candidate filtering or matching.
*   **Third-Party Integrations:** No integrations with external job boards or professional social networks (e.g., LinkedIn).

## Constraints & Technical Boundaries
*   **Platform:** Responsive web application optimized for mobile and desktop.
*   **Authentication:** Integration with university Single Sign-On (SSO) utilizing standard protocols such as OAuth2/OIDC (needs_confirmation).
*   **Media Storage:** Limit of 500MB per team for media uploads (needs_confirmation).
*   **User Access:** Access to profile creation is restricted exclusively to teams pre-vetted and admitted by the Gara Incubator administration (needs_confirmation).
*   **Rollout Strategy:** **Phased Rollout** starting with the core CMS before marketplace features.
## Business Rules
## Business Rules

| Rule ID | Condition | Trigger | Outcome | Scope | Exception |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| BR-01 | User authenticated via SSO and marked "Approved Founder" | Action to create project | Instantiate project in "Draft" state | New Projects | None |
| BR-02 | Profile in "Draft" state and all mandatory fields filled | Action to publish profile | Transition profile to "Published" state | Project Management | None |
| BR-03 | Project Owner or Admin initiates archive | Request to archive | Archive profile; delete active job postings | Project Management | None |
| BR-04 | User authenticated via SSO with active "Student" status | Action to apply for a role | Enable "Apply" button | Recruitment | Must have completed resume profile |
| BR-05 | Student has < 3 active applications | Submit application | Application processed | Recruitment | None |
| BR-06 | Student application declined by founder | Role decision | 30-day lockout from applying to that role at that startup | Recruitment | None |
| BR-07 | Published project has no logged action for 14 days | Automated system monitor | Flag "At-Risk"; notify owner | Compliance | None |
| BR-08 | Published project has no logged action for 30 days | Automated system monitor | Transition to "Suspended"; hide jobs | Compliance | None |
| BR-09 | Suspended project owner logs in and updates milestone | User interaction | Transition back to "Published"; restore jobs | Compliance | None |

### Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
*Summary of access for key roles (Guest, Student, Founder, Admin):*
- **View Profiles/Jobs:** All roles (Read-Only).
- **Manage Drafts:** Founder (Own Team), Admin (All Teams).
- **Post/Edit Jobs:** Startup Founder (Own only).
- **Vet Founders/Manage Administrative Flags:** Incubator Admin only.
- **Job Categorization:** Restricted to Engineering, Business, Design, or Marketing.

### Data Validation Constraints
- **Project Name:** 2-100 characters.
- **One-Sentence Pitch:** 10-150 characters.
- **Detailed Description:** Markdown, min 100 characters.
- **Milestone Requirement:** Min 1 active milestone required to publish.
- **Student Profile:** Must include contact link and uploaded PDF CV.
## Constraints, Assumptions, and Risks
# Constraints, Assumptions, and Risks: Gara Startup Project Showcase

This document outlines the hard operational limits (Constraints), foundational planning premises (Assumptions), and identified risks that govern the design, engineering, and rollout of the Gara Startup Project Showcase system.

## Constraints

| Category | Constraint | Details |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Technical** | University SSO | Authentication is restricted to university identity providers via OAuth2/OIDC. No native credential storage permitted. |
| **Technical** | Mobile-First Responsive | Must be fully accessible via standard mobile browsers (Safari, Chrome, etc.) without native app installation. |
| **Technical** | Storage Ceiling | Cumulative upload capacity is capped at 500MB per startup project (needs_confirmation) to control cloud costs. |
| **Regulatory** | Student Privacy | Compliance with university privacy policies; no public display of sensitive student data without explicit opt-in. |
| **Regulatory** | IP Protection | Startup project ownership remains with student teams; no forced exposure of trade secrets or patentable material. |
| **Operational** | No Human Moderation | Lack of staff necessitates automated validation rules and flagging; no daily human-in-the-loop review. |

## Assumptions

| Category | Assumption | Impact if Incorrect |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Behavioral** | Founder Maintenance | Founders will proactively manage their project roadmaps and profiles. |
| **Behavioral** | Internal Engagement | Students prefer internal university channels over external platforms (LinkedIn, etc.). |
| **Governance** | Admin Oversight | Incubator staff will monitor health metrics/flags at least twice weekly. |
| **Governance** | Pre-Vetted Registry | Administration will maintain an accurate, current registry of admitted teams (needs_confirmation). |
| **Integration** | API Accessibility | University IT will provide necessary API/sandbox access without significant delay (needs_confirmation). |
| **Infrastructure** | Hosting Stability | Internal university nodes possess sufficient compute/network resources to host the platform (needs_confirmation). |
